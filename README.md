## Standards

- [PMBOK Guide – Eighth Edition](https://www.pmi.org/standards/pmbok) - PMI's standard released in November 2025, with six principles, seven performance domains and expanded coverage of AI, PMOs and procurement.
- [ISO/IEC 42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) - The first international standard for an AI management system, setting requirements for any organization that develops, provides or uses AI.

## Certifications

- [PMP Exam Update](https://www.pmi.org/certifications/project-management-pmp/new-exam) - The exam updated in July 2026, adding AI, sustainability and stakeholder engagement in project-based scenarios and reweighting the domains to People 33%, Process 41% and Business Environment 26%.

## Governance and Regulation

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) - Voluntary framework for managing AI risk, released in January 2023, with a Generative AI Profile added in July 2024 and a revision under way.
- [EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) - Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence, the binding legal text in the Official Journal.
